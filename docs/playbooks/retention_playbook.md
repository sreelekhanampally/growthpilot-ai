# GrowthPilot retention and growth playbook

Prioritize high-value customers whose churn probability is above 70%. Begin with a personal message that acknowledges their previous relationship, then offer a relevant product or modest loyalty benefit. Avoid blanket discounts when the customer still has strong organic purchase intent.

For loyal customers with purchase propensity above 70%, use a cross-sell message centered on the highest-ranked unseen product. Explain why it complements their purchase history. Limit the message to one primary recommendation and one alternative.

Dormant, low-value customers should enter a low-cost automated reactivation journey. Stop after two unanswered contacts. Champions with low churn risk should receive recognition, early access, and useful recommendations rather than aggressive promotions.

Every action must record an outcome: contacted, accepted, declined, no response, purchased, or churned. Revenue attributable to a completed action should be stored as outcome value. Review conversion and incremental value by action type each month.
